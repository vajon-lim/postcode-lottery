# Data Sources — Online Research

All data in this folder was sourced from publicly available Australian government and academic sources.
Collected: May 2026.

## Primary Sources

### AIHW — Rural and Remote Health (2025)
- URL: https://www.aihw.gov.au/reports/rural-remote-australians/rural-and-remote-health
- Files using this: hospitalization_by_remoteness.csv, mortality_by_remoteness.csv, health_risk_factors_by_remoteness.csv, cancer_screening_by_remoteness.csv, gp_visits_by_mm.csv, hero_key_stats.csv
- Data: Hospitalization rates, mortality, cancer screening, GP attendance, health risk factors by remoteness area (2023–24)

### ABS — Patient Experiences Survey 2024–25
- URL: https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/health/health-services/patient-experiences/latest-release
- Files using this: patient_experience_by_region_2024_25.csv, hero_key_stats.csv
- Data: GP wait times, dental access, ED visits, specialist access by geographic area

### RACGP — General Practice: Health of the Nation 2025
- URL: https://www.racgp.org.au/health-of-the-nation-2025/chapter-4-current-workforce-and-the-future/gp-distribution
- Files using this: gp_fte_per_100k_by_mm.csv, hero_key_stats.csv
- Data: GP FTE per 100,000 by MM category 2019–2024

### AIHW — Survey of Health Care
- URL: https://www.aihw.gov.au/reports/rural-remote-australians/survey-health-care-selected-findings-rural-remote/contents/summary
- Files using this: access_barriers_by_remoteness.csv, hero_key_stats.csv
- Data: Barriers to care by remoteness (lack of nearby providers, usual GP availability)

### AIHW — Health Workforce / Oral Health (2023)
- URL: https://www.aihw.gov.au/reports/dental-oral-health/oral-health-and-dental-care-in-australia/contents/dental-workforce
- Files using this: workforce_per_100k_by_remoteness.csv
- Data: Dentist FTE per 100,000 by remoteness

### ABS — 2021 Census + Versace et al. 2021 (Australian Journal of Rural Health)
- DOI: 10.1111/ajr.12805
- Files using this: population_by_remoteness.csv, population_by_mm.csv
- Data: Population distribution by ARIA and MM categories (2021 Census)

### AIHW — Public Hospital Beds (2023–24)
- URL: https://www.aihw.gov.au/hospitals/topics/hospital-resources/public-hospital-beds
- Data: 2.47 beds per 1,000 nationally; Remote 4.0, Major cities 2.4 beds per 1,000
